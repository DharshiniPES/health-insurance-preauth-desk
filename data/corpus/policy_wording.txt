1.0 COVERAGE PROVISIONS
-----------------------
[Clause SEC-COV-INP]
Section 1.0 - Inpatient Hospitalisation Care
If an Insured Person is hospitalised for a minimum period of 24 consecutive hours for a covered illness or accidental injury occurring during the Policy Period, the Company will indemnify the reasonable and customary medical expenses incurred, subject to the terms, conditions, waiting periods, and Sum Insured limits of this Policy. Covered expenses include Room Rent, Nursing expenses, Boarding charges, Intensive Care Unit (ICU) charges, Surgeon, Anesthetist, Medical Practitioner, Consultant fees, OT charges, Medicines and Drugs, and Diagnostic tests.

[Clause SEC-COV-DAYCARE]
Section 1.1 - Day Care Procedures
The Company will indemnify medical expenses incurred for Day Care Treatments / Procedures listed in the Day Care Schedule that require less than 24 hours of hospitalisation due to technological advancement, provided the procedure was performed under General or Local Anesthesia in a Hospital or Day Care Centre. Outpatient (OPD) procedures are expressly excluded.

[Clause SEC-COV-EMERGENCY]
Section 1.2 - Emergency Hospitalisation and Accidental Injury
Hospitalisation arising out of sudden, life-threatening emergency medical conditions or accidental bodily injury will be covered from Day 1 of policy inception without application of initial waiting periods, provided the admission is notified to the Insurer within 24 hours of hospitalisation. Medical emergency must be verified by attending emergency physician notes.

[Clause SEC-COV-MAT]
Section 1.3 - Maternity Expenses Benefit
The Company will indemnify medical expenses for hospitalisation related to childbirth (including normal delivery or Caesarean section) or medically necessary termination of pregnancy, up to a maximum limit of Rs. 50,000 for normal delivery and Rs. 75,000 for Caesarean section, per policy year. This benefit is available only after the continuous completion of the 24-month waiting period specified under Clause 2.4.

2.0 WAITING PERIODS
-------------------
[Clause SEC-WAIT-30D]
Section 2.1 - Initial 30-Day Waiting Period
A waiting period of 30 days from the inception date of the Policy applies to all claims, during which any illness, disease or medical condition diagnosed or treated is not admissible. This waiting period shall not apply to accidental bodily injury requiring emergency hospitalisation.

[Clause SEC-WAIT-24M-SPECIFIC]
Section 2.2 - 24-Month Waiting Period for Specified Illnesses and Surgeries
A specific waiting period of 24 consecutive months of continuous coverage under this policy applies to the following diseases, medical conditions, and surgical procedures, irrespective of whether they are pre-existing or contracted after policy inception:
(a) Cataract surgery and related ophthalmological interventions;
(b) Benign Prostatic Hypertrophy (BPH);
(c) Hernia of all types (inguinal, incisional, umbilical, femoral);
(d) Hydrocele, Varicocele, Spermatocele;
(e) Hysterectomy for menorrhagia, uterine fibromyoma or prolapse;
(f) Cholelithiasis and Cholecystitis (Gall bladder stones);
(g) Calculus of the kidney, ureter, or urinary bladder;
(h) Joint replacement surgeries (Knee, Hip, Shoulder), unless necessitated solely by accidental bodily injury;
(i) Piles, Anal Fissure, Fistula-in-ano;
(j) ENT disorders including Chronic Sinusitis, Tonsillitis, Tympanoplasty, Mastoidectomy, and Deviated Nasal Septum;
(k) Osteoarthritis, Gout, and degenerative joint diseases.
No claim will be payable for these listed conditions if hospitalisation occurs before 24 months of continuous policy coverage have elapsed.

[Clause SEC-WAIT-36M-PED]
Section 2.3 - 36-Month Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period
Any medical condition, ailment, injury or disease that was diagnosed by a physician, or for which medical advice or treatment was recommended or received within 48 months prior to the effective date of the policy issued by the insurer, will be covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage without break. If a pre-existing condition was not declared at proposal inception, the claim is liable to be rejected on grounds of non-disclosure and misrepresentation.

[Clause SEC-WAIT-24M-MAT]
Section 2.4 - 24-Month Maternity Waiting Period
Expenses related to maternity, pregnancy, childbirth, or voluntary termination of pregnancy are subject to a waiting period of 24 months of continuous coverage under the policy. Any hospitalisation for delivery or maternity care occurring prior to 24 completed months from the policy start date is inadmissible.

3.0 EXCLUSIONS
--------------
[Clause SEC-EXCL-PERM]
Section 3.0 - General Permanent Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable to make any payment for any expenses incurred in connection with or arising from:
(a) Cosmetic or aesthetic plastic surgery, treatments for beautification, or any elective enhancements;
(b) Experimental, unproven, investigative or unconventional therapies, including non-approved stem cell therapy;
(c) Intentional self-inflicted injury, suicide attempt, or participation in criminal or illegal acts;
(d) Substance abuse, alcoholism, drug addiction, and rehabilitation treatments;
(e) High-risk hazardous activities, extreme adventure sports, or military operations;
(f) Domiciliary hospitalisation, outpatient consultation, diagnostic tests without hospital admission;
(g) Infertility, assisted reproductive technology (IVF, IUI, ICSI), surrogacy, or sterilization procedures.

4.0 SUBLIMITS
-------------
[Clause SEC-SUBLIM-ROOM]
Section 4.1 - Room Rent and ICU Sub-Limits
Daily room rent, boarding and nursing charges are limited to a maximum of 1% of the Sum Insured per day for Standard Single / Private AC room. ICU / ICCU charges are limited to a maximum of 2% of the Sum Insured per day. If the insured occupies a room category whose daily rent exceeds the eligible limit, the insured shall bear proportionate deduction across all associated hospital charges (including doctor fees, OT charges, and surgical fees).

[Clause SEC-SUBLIM-CATARACT]
Section 4.2 - Cataract Surgery Specific Limit
Where covered after the completion of the mandatory 24-month waiting period specified in Clause 2.2, the Company's liability for Cataract surgery (including costs of intraocular lens, OT charges, and surgeon fees) is capped at a maximum of Rs. 40,000 per eye, or 10% of the Sum Insured, whichever is lower, per policy year.

[Clause SEC-SUBLIM-JOINT]
Section 4.3 - Joint Replacement Limit
Where admissible after completion of the 24-month waiting period in Clause 2.2, the maximum indemnification for Joint Replacement Surgery (Knee, Hip, or Shoulder) is capped at Rs. 2,50,000 per joint or 50% of the Sum Insured, whichever is lower.
